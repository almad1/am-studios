Page301 Privacy Policy

P.301
This policy explains what information is collected when you use Page301, who collects it, and how it is used. Page301 is a free app — advertising helps keep it that way. We do not collect personal information ourselves; this policy is primarily about the advertising partners we work with.
01 — Who we are

AM Studios

Page301 is published by AM Studios. If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us at allen175@gmail.com.

02 — What information is collected

We do not collect personal data directly

Page301 does not ask you to create an account, does not request your name, email address, or location, and does not store any information about you on our servers.

The app fetches live football scores from a proxy server we operate via Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare may log standard server-side request metadata (IP address, timestamp, request path) as part of normal infrastructure operation. We do not use this data to identify you and do not retain it beyond Cloudflare's standard log retention periods.

Our advertising partner, Google AdMob, may collect information as described in the section below.

03 — Advertising and third parties

Google AdMob

Page301 uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. AdMob may collect the following to serve and measure ads:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
  • Device type, operating system, and language
  • IP address (used to infer approximate location)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is handled under Google's privacy policy. You can review it at policies.google.com/privacy.

iOS — App Tracking Transparency

On iOS 14.5 and later, the app will ask for your permission before AdMob accesses your device's advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

EU / EEA users — Consent

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, the app will display a consent form before any personalised ads are shown. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time by reinstalling the app or via your device settings.

04 — Data retention

We hold no user data to retain

Because we do not collect personal information directly, there is no user data for us to retain or delete. For data held by Google AdMob, please refer to Google's data retention policies.

05 — Children

Page301 is not directed at children

Page301 is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

06 — Your rights

GDPR and data subject rights

If you are located in the EU or EEA, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you, and the right to object to processing.

Since Page301 does not itself hold personal data, most requests of this nature will need to be directed to Google. We are happy to help you understand your options — contact us at the address below.

07 — Changes

Updates to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date below. Continued use of Page301 after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
